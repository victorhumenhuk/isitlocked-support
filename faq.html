FAQ

Clear answers.

Does the app know my door is locked?

No. It records what you personally checked. Apple Home can report a compatible accessory state, but you must still accept it explicitly.

How are routines suggested?

A pinned routine wins first, then a matching local schedule, then the routine used most recently for that weekday and daypart. The app does not infer destinations.

Does the app track me continuously?

No. Departure reminders use Apple region entry and exit events around properties you configure. It retains no movement history.

What does an NFC tag store?

A compatible tag stores an opaque random token. The app keeps only a cryptographic hash linked to the expected item.

Can I use Free without an account?

Yes. Free supports one editable Leaving now routine and one local property without an account.

Where are photographs stored?

On your device or in your private iCloud structure when Plus sync is enabled. Thumbnails are blurred outside receipt detail by default.

Can system surfaces record an item?

Widgets, Siri, Shortcuts, Control Center and Live Activities only start, resume or navigate. The Watch app asks you to confirm each eligible item explicitly. On iPhone, one hold records ordinary checks. Photo, NFC and Apple Home items pause for their extra evidence step. None of these actions physically locks anything.
