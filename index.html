Door checklist for iPhone and Apple Watch

Know what you checked before you left.

You check the door and the stove as you leave, then hold one button. Later, when the doubt comes back, your phone shows what you checked and the exact time.

Get Is It Locked? on the App Store

Make your list

Add whatever you turn back to check, like the front door, the stove or the iron, and give each one an emoji.

Hold once as you leave

Check each item the way you always do, then press and hold the big green button. The app checks off your whole list and saves the exact time.

Look back when you wonder

Halfway down the street, open the app or look at the widget. It says what you checked and when, like "Checked at 8:46 AM. Clears at 4:46 PM."

Plus

A reminder as you leave

Get a reminder as you leave a saved place, or on the days and at the time you choose. There's no continuous tracking and no location history.

Plus

On your wrist

Continue a check and confirm each item on Apple Watch. Complications, the Smart Stack and Lock Screen widgets show your last check.

Plus

Your whole household

Share a place with the people you live with through iCloud, and see who checked what and when.
