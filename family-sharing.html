Sharing

Two kinds of sharing.

Apple Family Sharing

Eligible family members can receive Plus through their own verified App Store transaction only after the Account Holder enables Apple’s irreversible setting. It is currently disabled.

Household sharing

Household sharing sends selected property, routine, active session, item action and receipt records through a private CloudKit share. One member can start and another can finish.

Membership

The share creator remains the owner. Owners can remove participants or stop sharing. Participants can leave. The app does not claim ownership transfer.

Privacy

Entitlement, permissions, current device identity, raw NFC tokens, accessory bindings and unsaved text do not synchronize.
